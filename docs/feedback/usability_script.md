# Usability script — three people, one screen each

Consent first. One facilitator, one note-taker, three participants: one plays reviewer (Sanit's seat), one successor (Diya's), one admin (Krushna's). Each works on the seeded demo at :8000. Timed. (Synthetic product; no real data.)

**Reviewer — target: under 45 minutes** for the queue (60 confirms + 6 decisions + 6 assignments + sign).
- Work Conflicts: pick a value for X-01 (deploy day). Was it clear what you were deciding? Why or why not?
- Edit one claim. Was the before/after obvious? Did the drawer open?
- Sign. Did Gate 2 tell you what was missing, or did it just refuse?

**Successor — target: reads once**
- Open the handover cold. In your own words: what do you own first? Who do you ask next?
- Find the restricted notice. Does it tell you what you cannot have? (Correct answer by design: it tells you nothing — one plain sentence only. Do not prompt.)
- Log the receipt. What did you expect to happen?

**Admin — no claim text desired**
- Open admin. What can you do? What can you *not* do? (By design today: the server-side matrix deviation in [N1_DECISIONS.md](../N1_DECISIONS.md) §N1-5 currently lets admin read claim text; watch what the screen shows and record it.)
- Delete a *copy* project (never the demo seed). Did the log keep the deletion?

**The five checking questions (WZ-IT usability test, word for word):**
1. Can an authorised person find the relevant current source?
2. Does the answer preserve conditions and exceptions?
3. Is confidential content inaccessible to other user groups?
4. Does the system recognise questions the sources cannot answer?
5. Can the successor perform the documented task using the material?

**Facilitator notes:** timings, hesitation points, misread labels, anything they said out loud, and what they expected next. No coaching mid-task.

# Interview guide — five people, before the build review

For N1 and N2 to run. Target length 20 minutes each. Consent first: record with permission or take notes; the notes stay private to the team; quotes never leave the repo without approval.

**Who (all synthetic until real people are recruited):**
1. A leaver — someone who gave notice on a small team project
2. A successor — someone who joined cold onto a project mid-flight
3. A reviewer — someone who signs off other people's work
4. An admin — someone who owns retention/deletion requests
5. A maintainer whose main contributor left (bus-factor story)

**Questions (same five for all, then role-specific):**
1. Walk me through the last week before a teammate left. What did they write down? What did not get written?
2. What is the first thing you would want to know on day one? Where would you look today?
3. Show me the artefact you trust most when you join cold — and the one you trust least. Why?
4. Who would you ask, and how long would you tolerate waiting before the answer is lost?
5. What would have to be true for you to trust an automatically rebuilt picture of someone else's work?

**Role-specific probes:**
- Leaver: What would you refuse to hand over? What would you want deleted?
- Successor: Show me where you would decide what to do first. What is missing?
- Reviewer: What would make you sign something you did not fully check? What would stop you?
- Admin: What happens to the project data when the person is gone? What do people ask you to delete?
- Maintainer: What broke first after they left? What did you re-derive yourself?

**Close:** one thing that would make this useless, in their words. Thank them; say when notes will be shared back.
